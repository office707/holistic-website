✦ Our Story

We started with one simple idea: good care should feel connected.

Aurea grew out of a simple patient problem: too many people were collecting advice from different places, but no one was helping them turn it into one clear path.

So the clinic was built around continuity. Different practitioners, one rhythm of intake, explanation, treatment pacing, and follow-up.

12+ years refining how integrative care is explained and delivered
3 clinic areas organized around visit type, energy level, and treatment need
1 shared care philosophy across diagnostics, therapies, and follow-up
What guides decisions here
Context before conclusions

Symptoms matter, but so do routines, history, timing, and what life currently makes possible.

Clarity before complexity

Patients should understand why a recommendation exists before the plan becomes more detailed.

✦ How We Work

The clinic model is designed to reduce noise, not add more of it.

Rather than treating every appointment as a separate event, Aurea uses one shared framework from intake to follow-up.

What that means in practice

We move deliberately at the beginning so the next steps feel clearer and easier to keep up with.

Longer first conversations

We begin with history, patterns, context, and what has already been tried.

Recommendations in sequence

Plans are paced so patients know what matters now and what can wait.

01
Start with the whole picture

The intake centers symptoms, history, routines, and where the patient feels stuck.

02
Choose the right clinical path

We match the case to the right practitioner, diagnostics, or combination of specialties.

03
Build a plan that can hold

The goal is a plan people can understand and actually continue.

✦ Our Values

The principles that shape the atmosphere, decisions, and patient experience.

These are practical standards the team uses when listening, recommending, and following through.

01

Listen deeply

We make room for history, uncertainty, and nuance before simplifying the case too quickly.

02

Explain clearly

Patients should understand what matters now, what can wait, and why the plan is structured that way.

03

Work collaboratively

Different specialties can support the same case without the clinic feeling fragmented.

04

Stay practical

Care only helps when it can be sustained, so we pace recommendations for real routines.
