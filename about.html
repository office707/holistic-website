Why Norbu exists

Less guesswork.
More context.

Starting a search for complementary care can raise more questions than answers. Which approaches are listed near you? What does a practice focus on? Where can you check the original information?

Norbu gives you a place to begin. Browse directory profiles, compare the details available and follow the links to the practice and its original source. Your decision stays yours.
