What Norbu does right now.

Norbu helps visitors browse acupuncture profiles in The Netherlands and continue to Google Maps for location details and any reviews available on Google.

Open finderTake the quiz
Simple directory Profiles by city and focus area External Google Maps links No booking or on-site reviews
01

Can I book an appointment through Norbu?

No. Norbu does not handle appointments, contact requests, payments, messages, or booking calendars yet. The current version helps visitors compare profiles and continue to Google Maps.

02

Where can visitors see reviews?

Reviews are viewed on Google. Norbu does not collect, store, moderate, score, or publish its own reviews on the website.

03

Are exact clinic addresses included?

Not yet. Current map buttons open Google Maps searches by city and focus area. Exact addresses can be added once provider data is confirmed.

04

Does the quiz recommend treatment?

No. The quiz only helps visitors choose a search direction, such as fertility, pain, stress and sleep, or Traditional Chinese Medicine. It is not medical advice.

05

Are therapists ranked or verified?

No. Norbu does not rank therapists, create review scores, or claim verification in this version. Visitors should compare profile information and check external Google details themselves.

06

What can be added later?

Confirmed provider profiles, exact addresses, richer city pages, contact details, provider-managed updates, and booking can be added once the core directory is working.

Good to know

Norbu is an orientation tool.

It is not medical advice and it does not replace a consultation with a qualified professional. The goal is to make the first search step easier to understand.
