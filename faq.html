01What exactly is Norbu?

Norbu is a directory for exploring acupuncture and complementary practice listings in the Netherlands. Compare available profile information and follow the links to the practice, its source listing or Google Maps.

02Can I book through Norbu?

Direct booking is not available. Open the practice’s website or Google Maps listing to check contact details and ask the practice about appointments.

03Are practitioners independently verified?

Inclusion in the directory does not mean Norbu has independently verified qualifications or suitability. Check registration, training and treatment options directly with the practice.

04Where can I see reviews?

Use the Google Maps link on a practice profile to check available Google reviews. Google manages those ratings and reviews; Norbu does not publish its own patient reviews.

05Does the map show an exact practice location?

The map uses the location details supplied in a profile. Some listings have incomplete addresses or open a Google search rather than a verified point. Confirm the address with the practice before visiting.

06Does the quiz recommend a treatment?

The quiz suggests a direction for browsing the directory. It does not diagnose, assess suitability or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

07Where do the profile details come from?

Profiles contain information from the external directory sources shown on each listing. Open the original source or practice website to check the latest details.

08What if information is missing or outdated?

Check the source listing and the provider’s own website. Confirm important details directly with the practice, including its location, qualifications and appointment availability.
