Profiles, maps, reviews

What this first version does and does not do.

Norbu is currently a simple acupuncture directory prototype for The Netherlands. It shows sample therapist profiles, Google Maps search links, and local review functionality.

Current scope

Limits

Clear MVP boundaries

These answers keep the product honest until real provider data, moderation, and contact flows are added.

Profiles

Are these verified providers?

No. The current profiles are sample MVP entries used to demonstrate the directory structure.

Maps

Are exact clinic addresses included?

No. The map buttons open Google Maps searches by city and specialization. Exact addresses can be added later.

Reviews

Are reviews public or moderated?

No. Reviews in this MVP are saved locally in the visitor's browser. They are useful for testing the review flow only.

Booking

Can users book through Norbu?

No. Booking, messaging, payments, and scheduling are not part of this version.

Medical

Does Norbu recommend a treatment?

No. The site does not diagnose, rank therapists, or recommend care. It only helps visitors compare profile information.

Next

What can be added after the MVP?

Provider onboarding, claimed profiles, exact addresses, review moderation, and optional contact details can be added later.
