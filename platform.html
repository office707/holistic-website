✦ Roles & Permissions

A simple role-based structure before a full patient portal even exists.

The internal system can stay lightweight now, while still separating ownership, responsibilities, and access levels.

Owner / Admin Everything from settings to approvals.

Oversees platform structure, access, scheduling rules, and overall clinic operations.

Practitioner Focused patient and schedule visibility.

Sees appointments, selected notes, and resources relevant to their own practice area.

Content Editor Journal, FAQ, and homepage updates.

Manages editorial sections without touching sensitive operational data.

Front Desk Requests, messages, and booking flow.

Handles incoming inquiries, scheduling coordination, and status updates throughout the day.

Phase 1

Internal CRM

Doctors, content, room bookings, and contact form requests all in one place.

Phase 2

Secure practitioner login

Role-aware access for doctor-specific scheduling, notes, and workflow updates.

Phase 3

Patient portal

Only if the clinic truly needs private user messaging, saved recommendations, or account-based progress tracking.
