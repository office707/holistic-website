What Norbu does and does not do

Norbu helps visitors orient themselves, compare profile information, and continue to Google Maps. It is not a booking platform or a medical recommendation service.

No booking yetAppointments, calendars, contact requests, and payments are not part of this version.
No medical recommendationThe quiz suggests a search direction only. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
No on-site reviewsNorbu does not collect, publish, or score reviews. Reviews stay on Google.
