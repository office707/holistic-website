More questions? Visit the FAQ →
What exactly is Norbu?

Norbu is a directory for exploring acupuncture and complementary practice listings in the Netherlands. It brings together available profile information, therapy search terms and links to original sources.

Can I book an appointment through Norbu?

Direct booking is not available yet. Open the practice’s website or Google Maps listing to check contact details and ask the practice about appointments.

Are the practitioners independently verified?

Directory entries come from listed external sources. Inclusion does not mean Norbu has independently verified a practitioner’s qualifications. Check registration, training and suitability directly with the provider.

Where can I find reviews?

Use the Google Maps link on each practice profile to check available Google reviews. Reviews and ratings are managed by Google. Norbu does not publish its own patient reviews.

Does the quiz recommend a treatment?

The quiz offers a starting direction for browsing. It does not diagnose, assess suitability or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
