Common questions before starting care.

Clear answers on how treatment starts, what kind of care you can expect, and how a broader plan gets built over time.

Do I need to know which therapy I want before booking?

No. Most patients start with an assessment, then we decide whether functional medicine, acupuncture, nutrition, bodywork, or a combination makes the most sense.

Is this more wellness or more medical?

It is deliberately both. The atmosphere stays calm and restorative, but the thinking is clinical, structured, and based on symptoms, history, and measurable follow-up.

Can I come for one issue and build a broader plan later?

Yes. Many patients begin with fatigue, digestion, cycle care, or stress, then expand into a longer-term care pathway once the first phase is stable.
